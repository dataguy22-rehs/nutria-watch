3

Submit the sighting through our GIS reporting form.

Why report nutria?

Nutria are invasive rodents that can damage wetlands, waterways, levees and other habitat. Early detection helps wildlife agencies respond to suspected sightings.

Never approach, touch, or attempt to capture wildlife.
